Good to Know

Questions Shops Ask Us

Does the tool truck really come to my shop?
Yes. That's the whole model. Tool Co runs a mobile tool truck on a regular route through Edinburg, Pharr, San Juan, Alamo, and Weslaco. Get on the route and the truck stops at your shop, so you and your crew shop right there.
Are you a Cornwell store?
Tool Co is an independent, authorized Cornwell Quality Tools franchise. The tools are Cornwell. The truck, the route, and the service are ours. We're not Cornwell corporate, and there's no storefront to visit; the truck is the store.
Which cities do you run?
Edinburg is home base, and the route covers Weslaco, Pharr, Alamo, and San Juan. If your shop sits just outside those cities, reach out and ask. The route has some give in it.
How does tool financing work?
Financing and payment options are available on tool purchases. The specifics depend on the shop and the purchase, so they're worked out with your dealer in person, with no pressure. Reach out and we'll go over the options before the truck comes by.
What happens if a tool needs warranty service?
Tell your dealer at the next stop. We help you handle Cornwell's warranty and service process so you're not chasing it on your own. Coverage depends on the tool, and your dealer will walk you through what applies.
How do I see this month's catalog?
Cornwell's online catalog is live year-round at the View Inventory link, and a new flyer comes out every month. Browse ahead, note the part numbers, and your dealer will have them on the truck for your stop.
How do I get on the route?
Call, text, or send the contact form. Tell us where the shop is and what your crew runs. We'll add your stop and let you know which day the truck comes by.
