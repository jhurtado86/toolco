What's in It

The Flyer Is the Month. The Catalog Is the Line.

Two different things, and it helps to keep them straight. The monthly flyer is the short one: that month's promotions and new releases, the tools worth a look right now. The online catalog is the whole Cornwell line, from a single socket to a full roll cab, live all year.

How It Works on the Route

From the Catalog to Your Bay in Three Steps

Nothing to ship to your door and nothing to figure out alone. The catalog is where you look. The truck is where you buy.

1

Browse

Open the online catalog or this month's flyer on your phone, at the bench, whenever. Find what you want and note the part number.

2

Tell your dealer

Text it, call it in, or say it at the truck. Your dealer checks it and adds it to your stop.

3

It rides in

The order comes out on the truck at your next stop. Pay at the truck, and if you'd rather spread it out, ask about financing and payment options.
