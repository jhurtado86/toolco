San Juan, TX · Between Pharr and Alamo

San Juan Is the Stop Between Stops

On paper San Juan is the town you pass through between Pharr and Alamo. On the Tool Co route it's a stop of its own. The truck comes off I-2 / US-83, runs Business 83 and Raul Longoria Road, and works the shops there before moving east. Nobody in San Juan has to catch the truck in the next town over.

San Juan is close to home base, which shows in how the stop works. A tool held between visits, a catalog order, or a warranty question doesn't ride around the Valley for a week. It comes back on the next San Juan day, and that day is never far off. The shops here are mostly independents, and the route is built for exactly that.

Coverage Across San Juan

Where the Truck Stops in San Juan

San Juan's shops sit on the highway, the old main street, and the north–south road through town. These are the corridors the route runs on the San Juan stop.

Expressway 83 / I-2 frontage

The route highway through San Juan. Shops along the frontage roads between the Pharr and Alamo exits are first on the stop.

Business 83 through town

The old highway through the middle of San Juan: independent repair shops, tire shops, and parts counters.

Raul Longoria Road

The north–south road through San Juan, from the expressway up toward FM 495. Shops and yards along the corridor.

FM 495 and the north side

The east–west road across the north of town, shared with Pharr and Alamo, and the shops off it.

Good to Know

Questions About the Tool Truck in San Juan

Does the Cornwell tool truck come to shops in San Juan?
Yes. San Juan is a stop of its own on the route, between Pharr and Alamo on I-2 / US-83. Get your shop on the route and the truck stops at your lot on a set day.
Which parts of San Juan does the route cover?
All of it. Shops along the Expressway 83 frontage roads, Business 83 through town, Raul Longoria Road, and FM 495 on the north side are all reachable on the route. If your shop is inside San Juan, you're covered.
How do I get a San Juan shop on the route?
Call or text (956) 450-7963, or send the contact form. Tell us where the shop is and what your crew runs. We add your stop and let you know which day the truck comes by.
How fast can I get a tool that isn't on the truck?
San Juan is a short run from home base, so a catalog order or a held tool comes back on the next San Juan day. Note the part number, text your dealer, and it rides along.
My shop is on the Pharr or Alamo side of the line. Which stop am I on?
Whichever is closer to your door. The route runs Pharr, San Juan, and Alamo back to back on I-2, so reach out with your address and we'll put you on the stop that fits.
